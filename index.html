Welcome to Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center. Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) offers Montessori education that helps children learn at their own pace, build independence, and grow into caring members of the community. With five campuses across Metro Manila and Pampanga, OBMC is proud to celebrate 60 years of nurturing young learners.
